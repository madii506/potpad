01 — the rule

One pot. One winner. Every hour.

No application, no allowlist, no discretion. The hour decides, the chain pays, the receipt is public.

01

Post about $POT

Anything. A thread, a chart, a joke. It only has to mention the coin and be public.

02

The most-viewed post in the hour takes the pot

Views are read live from X at the top of each hour. Not likes, not followers — views, the only number that measures how far it actually travelled.

03

The dollars land before you know you won

X Money sends dollars to any handle for free. No wallet to connect, no code to paste in your bio, no window before it burns. Every other pad says only you can claim. This one has nothing to claim.

04

The payout posts itself

Each win goes out as a public post naming the handle, the post, the views and the amount, linked to a receipt. Nobody can claim an hour that never happened.

05 — the spec

How an hour is actually settled.

Written down so it cannot be changed quietly. If any of this ever moves, the change is a post with a date on it.

ELIGIBLEa post must
  • be public and readable through X's own endpoints
  • name $POT in its text
  • have been posted inside the hour being settled
  • report a view count — without one it cannot be ranked
  • come from a handle not already paid today
NOT ELIGIBLEa post that
  • is private, protected, or deleted before settlement
  • was edited after the hour closed
  • comes from an account created inside the same hour
  • is a repost — the original wins, not the repost
  • comes from the $POT account itself
SETTLEMENT ORDERon the hour, utc
T+0

The hour closes

Posts made after this second belong to the next hour, not this one.

T+1m

Views are read

Every eligible post is read once. The number recorded is the number at that moment and it is the number published — later drift never changes a settled hour.

T+2m

The winner is ranked

Highest views takes it. A tie goes to whichever post was published earlier. If the top handle has already been paid today, it passes to the next post down.

T+3m

The dollars send

Through X Money, to the handle, in dollars. The recipient does nothing.

T+4m

The receipt posts

A public post carrying the handle, the post, the view count and the amount. That post is the record, and it is the row that appears in the rundown.

06 — the difference

Everywhere else, and here.

EVERYWHERE ELSE
  • the fee goes to one handle, picked once by whoever launched it
  • the money is escrowed until you claim it
  • you post a code in your bio to prove it is you
  • a window runs out and the money goes back
  • you need a wallet, and the seed phrase that comes with it
  • you find out you were owed something months later
HERE
  • the fee goes to whoever the timeline actually watched, every hour
  • there is no escrow, because there is no claim
  • you prove nothing — you already posted from the account
  • nothing expires, because nothing is waiting
  • you need an X account, which you are reading this on
  • you find out because dollars appeared

09 — faq

The awkward questions, first.

Is there a mint address yet?

No. $POT has no mint address at the time of writing. When one exists it will be on this page. Anyone posting an address before it appears here is scamming you.

Why is everything on this page a dash?

Because the coin does not exist yet. A dash is the honest number — a zero would imply we looked and found nothing, and we have not looked at all. Every figure fills in by itself once there is a mint address and a pair.

Do I need a wallet to get paid?

No. X Money sends dollars to any X handle for free, so the money arrives in your X Money balance. Nothing to connect, no code to paste, no deadline after which it disappears.

What stops one account winning every hour?

X Money is rate-limited to one payment per person per day. That is the rail's rule, not ours — and it is exactly why there is one winner per hour rather than a spray. An account already paid today is skipped and the pot goes to the next post down.

What if two posts tie on views?

The one published earlier takes it. Ties break on timestamp, never on judgement.

Can I win with a post about something else?

No. The post has to name $POT. Beyond that there are no rules about what it says — including whether it is complimentary.

Where does the money come from?

Trading fees, and nowhere else. No treasury, no allocation, no outside funding. If nobody trades, the pot is empty and this page will say so.

Can I check the payouts without trusting you?

Yes. Each payout is a public post and a receipt, and the fee income derives from on-chain volume you can read yourself. The model above does that arithmetic in front of you and prints its own working; the docs publish the settlement order in full.
