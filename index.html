01 — the rule

One pot. One winner. Every hour.

There is no application, no allowlist and no discretion. The hour decides, the chain pays, and the receipt is public.

I

Post about $POT

Anything. A thread, a chart, a joke. It only has to mention the coin and be public.

II

The most-viewed post in the hour takes the pot

Views are read live from X at the top of each hour. Not likes, not followers — views, which is the only number that measures how far it actually travelled.

III

The dollars land before you know you won

X Money sends dollars to any handle for free. There is no wallet to connect, no code to paste in your bio and no window before it burns. Every other pad on this chain says only you can claim. This one has nothing to claim.

IV

The payout posts itself

Each win goes out as a public post naming the handle, the post, the views and the amount, linked to a receipt. Nobody can claim an hour that never happened.

06 — faq

The awkward questions, first.

Why does everything on this page show a dash?

Because the coin does not exist yet. A dash is the honest number — a zero would imply we looked and found nothing, and we have not looked at all. Every figure here fills in by itself once there is a contract address and a pair.

Do I need a wallet to get paid?

No. X Money sends dollars to any X handle for free, so the money arrives in your X Money balance. You do not connect anything, you do not paste a code anywhere, and there is no deadline after which it disappears.

What stops one account winning every hour?

X Money is rate-limited to one payment per person per day. That is not our rule, it is the rail's — and it is exactly why there is one winner per hour rather than a spray. An account that has already been paid today is skipped and the pot goes to the next post.

What counts as a post about $POT?

A public post that names the coin. Replies count, quotes count, threads count as their first post. Private and deleted posts cannot be read, so they cannot win.

Where does the money actually come from?

Trading fees, and nowhere else. There is no treasury, no allocation and no outside funding. If nobody trades, the pot is empty and the page will say so.

Can I see the payouts without trusting you?

Yes. Each payout is a public post and a receipt, and the fee income is derived from on-chain volume you can read yourself: 24h volume × 0.7%. The model above does that arithmetic in front of you.
